## Research Expertise
**Research statement**: Designs robust, physically grounded AI systems by building the structure of the real world (symmetry, physics, and embodiment) into modern foundation models.

**Primary Areas**:
1. Symmetry & Robustness: geometric deep learning, symmetry-aware foundation models, provably robust AI systems
2. Scientific Machine Learning: neural operators, physics foundation models, multiphysics and weather surrogates
3. Multimodal & Embodied AI: multimodal LLMs and agents, efficient vision-language-action models (VLAs)

**Key Contributions**:
- Tunable soft equivariance for pretrained vision foundation models with provable bounds on equivariance violation (CVPR 2026)
- Local scale equivariance for pretrained backbones via a latent deep equilibrium canonicalizer (ICCV 2025)
- Group downsampling with equivariant anti-aliasing for G-CNNs (ICLR 2025)
- CoDA-NO: codomain-attention neural operators pretrained across multiphysics PDEs with few-shot transfer (NeurIPS 2024)
- Truly scale-equivariant deep nets with Fourier layers (NeurIPS 2023)
- U-NO: memory-efficient U-shaped neural operators (TMLR 2023)
- SkipVLA: pairs VLAs with classical motion planning, cutting task-completion time by 46% and energy by 52% on a physical robot arm (2026, under review)
